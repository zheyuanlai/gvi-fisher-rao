Affine invariance of the Gaussian Fisher--Rao flow. (a) The optimizer-whitened covariance band is entered after the predicted $\log[1/(\beta_\star\lambda_{0,\star})]$ of Theorem 2.9, over $\kappa\in\{10,10^2,10^3\}$ and $\lambda_0\in\{10^{-2},\dots,10^{-12}\}$. (b) Transformed Fisher--Rao iterates agree with the transported reference at roundoff level over eight decades of coordinate conditioning; FB--GVI does not. (c) On the anisotropic Gaussian of Diao et al., where $\kappa=10^9$ but $\kappa_\star=1$, both Fisher--Rao schemes reach machine precision at their own best swept step while FB--GVI remains limited by the original-coordinate conditioning.
