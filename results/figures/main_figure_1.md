# main_figure_1 caption draft

Affine invariance of the Gaussian Fisher--Rao flow. (a) The optimizer-whitened covariance band is entered after the predicted $\log[1/(\beta_\star\lambda_{0,\star})]$ of Theorem 2.9, over $\kappa\in\{10,10^2,10^3\}$ and $\lambda_0\in\{10^{-2},\dots,10^{-12}\}$. (b) Transformed Fisher--Rao iterates agree with the transported reference to within roundoff over eight decades of coordinate conditioning: the residual grows like the conditioning of the change of variables itself, from $10^{-15}$ at $K=1$ to $10^{-9}$ at $K=10^8$, which is floating-point amplification through an ill-conditioned map rather than a loss of equivariance. FB--GVI, whose Bures--Wasserstein geometry is not affine invariant, departs by an $O(1)$ amount as soon as the map is non-orthogonal. (c) On the anisotropic Gaussian of Diao et al., where $\kappa=10^9$ but $\kappa_\star=1$, both Fisher--Rao schemes reach machine precision at their own best swept step while FB--GVI remains limited by the original-coordinate conditioning.
