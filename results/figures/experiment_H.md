# experiment_H caption draft

Sticking-the-landing cancellation on a Gaussian target initialized with the matched covariance $C_0=C_\star$ and a mismatched mean, $B=1$, 30 paired seeds. The Fisher--Rao STL schemes are seed-independent to floating-point precision, because the STL mean noise is proportional to $C_n-C_\star$ and vanishes pathwise once the covariance is matched (Corollary 4.20); S--FB--GVI retains its native estimator noise. This compares complete algorithms together with their native estimators, not geometry alone.

Underlying data: `results/processed/experiment_H.csv`.
