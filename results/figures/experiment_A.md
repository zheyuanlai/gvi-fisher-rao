Fisher-Rao covariance burn-in on Gaussian targets with $d=20$, $\kappa\in\{10,10^2,10^3\}$ and $\lambda_0\in\{10^{-2},\dots,10^{-12}\}$, step $\Delta t=0.1$. (a) The measured entry time of the optimizer-whitened covariance band $\lambda_{\min}(\widetilde C_n)\ge 1/(2\beta_{\star})$ follows the predicted $\log[1/(\beta_{\star}\lambda_{0,{\star}})]$ envelope of Theorem 2.9. (b) The entry time is independent of the original-coordinate condition number $\kappa$, as affine invariance requires. (c) The hardest start shows the covariance reaching the band before the energy gap contracts.
